Why FairFold

🛡

Private

Personal details are removed before AI screening. Employers see a per-employer alias, not your identity, until you are shortlisted.

🔍

Explainable

Every match and every screening suggestion shows the reasons behind it, so you can check the evidence instead of trusting a number.

✳

Affordable

Free to start, with simple plans for teams that hire regularly. Candidates never pay to apply.

How it works

  1. Step 1

    Create a profile or post a role

    Candidates describe what they are looking for; employers describe the job.

  2. Step 2

    AI suggests, with reasons

    Screening works on anonymised text and every result carries its evidence.

  3. Step 3

    People decide

    A human shortlists or rejects with a written reason, and every action is audited.
