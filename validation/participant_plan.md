## Target Sample Sizes

| Participant type | Target count | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Recruiters (HR generalists) | 2 | Provides HR perspective on processes and pain points |
| Hiring managers | 2 | Provides hiring decision perspective |
| Additional recruiters | 1–2 | As available, covers more companies |
| **Total recruiters** | **5–6 minimum** | Matches §1.3.4 plan, enables theme saturation |
| Job seekers (candidate-side check) | 3–5 | Validate candidate UX decisions |

### Company selection criteria

**Primary target: Software companies and BPOs in Bangladesh.**

| Criterion | Target |
|---|---|
| Industry | Software development, BPO services, IT hiring |
| Company size | Mix: 10–50 employees (small) and 50–500 (mid-size) |
| Location | Dhaka, Chittagong, Sylhet (primary), plus remote-first companies |
| Recruiting volume | "100+ applicants per role" (per prd.md §6.2) |
| Current tools | Spreadsheets, free ATS, or none formally |

**Recruiter role mix:**
- At least one HR generalist (handles end-to-end hiring)
- At least one hiring manager (makes final decisions)

## Screener Questions (for participant recruitment)

**For recruiters/hiring managers:**

1. What type of company do you work for, and what roles do you usually hire for?
2. Approximately how many applicants do you receive per role?
3. What is your current screening process? (Tools, steps, time)
4. What is your budget for recruitment tools? (Current monthly spend, if any)
5. Do you use any AI or automated screening tools today? If so, which ones?
6. Would you be willing to participate in a 45-minute interview about hiring challenges?

**For job seekers:**

1. What is your current employment status? (Currently employed, seeking work, recently rejected)
2. What type of roles are you applying for?
3. Have you experienced a hiring process in Bangladesh or similar markets recently?
4. Did you receive a skills test or feedback from any of those applications?
5. Would you be willing to participate in a 20-minute session about your job search experience?

## Consent Script

**Read aloud before each session:**

> "Thank you for agreeing to participate in this research session. This is a FairFold project interview about hiring processes. It will last about 45 minutes for recruiters or 20 minutes for job seekers.

> We will write down your answers, and we may quote you in aggregate (like '5 of 6 participants said...') without using your name. Your notes will be stored anonymously.

> This is not a hiring process, and nothing we discuss affects any current or future job applications. There will be no follow-up sales calls.

> You can stop at any time, skip any question, or ask us to delete your notes. Do you have any questions before we begin?"

**Recording policy:** Written notes only. No audio or video recording.

**Anonymity of results:** All findings are reported with invented identifiers (e.g., "Company A," "Recruiter B"). No names, emails, or specific company details appear in any analysis.

## Follow-up opportunities

If a participant is enthusiastic about the concept:

1. **Second meeting:** Discuss specific feature designs or pricing
2. **Share a job description:** For live demo of screening flow
3. **Pilot participation:** Run a real job through the screening flow
4. **Letter of intent:** Commit to continuing as a customer post-validation

See `pre_commitment_ask.md` for the commitment ladder.
