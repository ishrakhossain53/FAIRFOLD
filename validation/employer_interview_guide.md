# Employer Interview Guide — 45 Minutes

**Document:** `validation/employer_interview_guide.md`  
**Date:** 2026-10-13  
**Purpose:** Guide for recruiters and hiring managers on employer-side validation interviews.

---

## Session Setup

- **Duration:** 45 minutes
- **Format:** Written notes only (no recording)
- **Participants:** Recruiter and/or Hiring Manager
- **Note-taker:** Research team member
- **Code assignment:** Assign anonymous code (R01, R02, etc.) after session ends

---

## Warm-up (5 minutes)

> Read consent script first, then continue:

> "Thanks again for participating. Can you tell me in one sentence what FairFold does, based on what you've heard?"

> "What's your role at [Company], and how long have you been in that position?"

> "How many roles have you been responsible for hiring for in the last 6 months?"

---

## Core Questions (30 minutes)

### 1. Current screening process

> "Walk me through how you screened the last role you filled. How many applicants did you receive, how much time did it take, and what tools did you use?"

**Probes:**
- "What percentage of applicants did you actually interview?"
- "What was the hardest part of that screening process?"
- "Did you use any skills tests or assessments? How?"

### 2. Internal suggestions and networking

> "How often do you have candidates suggested internally (by colleagues, managers, connections)? What do you do with those suggestions?"

**Probes:**
- "Do you give them preference over other applicants?"
- "How do you track where they came from?"

### 3. Skills testing practices

> "Do you test skills before interviews? Why or why not? What kind of tests do you use?"

**Probes:**
- "How long does a typical skills test take?"
- "Who decides the pass/fail mark?"
- "What happens if a candidate doesn't pass?"

### 4. Initial reaction to anonymised screening (W30, W37)

> "I want to show you a screen that employers would see. [Show wireframe of Ranked Applications with Alias Badge, W37, or Candidate Review with Stripped Text View, W41]."

> "This is how FairFold would show you ranked candidates. Your own name and the candidate's name are hidden. What's your first reaction to this?"

**Probes:**
- "Could you identify candidates you'd want to shortlist from this view?"
- "Is there anything critical missing that you need to make a decision?"
- "How would this compare to your current tool?"

### 5. The assessment gate (W39, W42)

> "If a job requires a skills assessment, FairFold would show this status on each candidate. [Show wireframe of Assessment Gate Pill]."

> "This role requires candidates to pass an assessment before they can be shortlisted on score alone. How would you use this information?"

**Probes:**
- "Would you set different difficulty levels for different roles?"
- "How would this affect your hiring timeline?"
- "Would you want to require this for most roles, or only some?"

### 6. AI explanations and trust (W41, S13)

> "For each candidate, FairFold shows the exact resume text that supports the score. [Show Score Card component from W41]."

> "Would you trust a score that shows exactly why a candidate matched? What would make you distrust it?"

**Probes:**
- "How important is it to see the actual evidence for each claim?"
- "What would make you override a ranking?"
- "How often do you think AI gets this right vs. wrong?"

### 7. Override recording (W32, W11)

> "If you want to shortlist a candidate ranked below your cut-off, FairFold requires you to record why. [Show Override Reason Dialog, W11]."

> "The system makes this decision visible and count. What would make you comfortable with this requirement?"

**Probes:**
- "Would you actually use this feature, or would it slow you down?"
- "What if you disagree with the AI ranking but don't want to admit why?"
- "How would you feel about your overrides being tracked for team learning?"

### 8. Cost estimate and confirmation (W14, S14)

> "Before processing applications, FairFold shows the estimated cost and requires confirmation. [Show Cost Estimate Modal, W34]."

> "The free tier shows $0.00. How valuable would this be for your hiring budget?"

**Probes:**
- "What's your current cost per hire, including tool subscriptions?"
- "How would you measure ROI on a screening tool?"
- "What would make you upgrade from the free tier?"

### 9. What would stop you using this?

> "No product is perfect. What would make you refuse or stop using FairFold after trying it?"

**Probes:**
- "Is there anything here that feels like a dealbreaker?"
- "What would you need to see to trust this tool?"
- "Would your team be comfortable with AI making recommendations?"

---

## Close (5 minutes)

> "Thank you for these insights. Before we end:

> 1. Is there anything we haven't asked that you think is important for employers to know about screening?

> 2. Would you be open to a second, shorter session to test specific feature designs or pricing?"

> "We will assign you an anonymous code and write up the findings. Do you have any questions?"

---

## Probes are only to be used when participants leave something unexplained.

## Warning: Do not lead with questions like:

- "Wouldn't you agree this is better than your current tool?"
- "Don't you think AI screening saves time?"
- "Isn't it important to be fair?"

These invite agreement and turn the interview into confirmation of the PRD.
